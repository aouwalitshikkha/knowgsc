My site pages are discovered not index. What can be the problem?
G

Your pages are marked Discovered – currently not indexed in Google Search Console. This means Google found the URLs but has not added them to its index yet.

Definition

Discovered – currently not indexed means Google discovered the page through discovery signals (such as your sitemap or internal links) but has not indexed it. It does not mean the page is broken — it means indexing has not happened.

Possible causes · labeled as hypotheses

  • Hypothesis Sitemap omission: the URL may be missing or incorrect in your submitted sitemap.
  • Hypothesis Weak internal discovery signals: few or no internal links point to the page, so crawl priority may be low.
  • Hypothesis Crawl accessibility: intermittent issues such as HTTP 504 may prevent reliable crawling.
